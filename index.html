About the Division

The Fisheries & Sealing Division supports the sustainable development of Nunavut's fisheries, sealing, and marine resources. Operating under the Department of Community Services, the Division delivers programs that strengthen harvesting capacity, expand commercial fisheries, and support Nunavummiut who depend on the land and sea.

Our programs serve individual harvesters, Hunters and Trappers Organizations (HTOs), Inuit organizations, businesses, and researchers across the Qikiqtaaluk, Kivalliq, and Kitikmeot regions. We administer funding programs, coordinate the RV Nuliajuk research vessel, and work closely with communities on fisheries management and development.

For more information, visit gov.nu.ca/en/industry/fisheries-and-sealing.
